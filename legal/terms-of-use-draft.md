## 4. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES

### 4.1 Voice Capture
Tap mic button to record voice memos. Audio transmitted to OpenAI GPT-4o-mini for transcription and structuring into notes, tasks, reminders, calendar events. **By using voice capture, you explicitly consent to recording and AI processing of your voice.**

### 4.2 Photo and Image Capture (PixNote)
Capture photos or upload screenshots. Images transmitted to Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for AI visual analysis, text extraction, and structuring. **By using photo capture, you explicitly consent to AI processing of your images.**

### 4.3 Written Notes
Typed text processed by AI to extract structured information.

### 4.4 Smart Alarms and Reminders
App creates iOS local notifications and calendar events based on AI-extracted dates/times. **These are iOS local notifications, not a guaranteed alarm service.** See Section 11.

### 4.5 Apple Calendar Integration
App reads from and writes to Apple Calendar to create events and generate Daily Brief. See Section 11.

### 4.6 Daily Brief Emails
If opted in, App sends morning email summaries, 4 PM task nudges, and all-clear completion notifications. See Section 12.

### 4.7 Memory Stash and Link Stash
Searchable archives of notes (Memory Stash) and saved web links (Link Stash), including via iOS Share Extension.

### 4.8 AI Assistant
Personalized AI assistant ("Pip") interacts within the App and optionally via Telegram. Pip is an AI, not a human. Telegram interactions subject to Telegram's terms.
