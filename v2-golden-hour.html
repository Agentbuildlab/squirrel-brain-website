How it works

Three ways to capture. One brain that handles the rest.

Speak It

Tap once and talk. Squirrel Brain transcribes your voice, extracts tasks and deadlines, and sets alarms — all hands-free.

Snap It

Point your camera at a whiteboard, sticky note, receipt, or business card. AI reads it and turns every item into an action.

Stash It

Save links, screenshots, and notes into your Memory Stash. Get a 5 AM daily brief so nothing falls through the cracks.

For the field

Built for people who work outside a desk.
