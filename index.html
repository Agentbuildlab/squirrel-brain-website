☀️ Daily Brief

Two emails a day.
Zero noise.

Your squirrel already knows what you need to do. It just needs you to wake up.

Every morning at 5 AM, your daily brief lands in your inbox — already waiting when you wake up. No app to open. Just clarity on what matters today.

In the afternoon, your squirrel decides: if everything's done, you get an all-clear. If work's still pending, you get a 4 PM nudge with exactly what's left — nothing more.
